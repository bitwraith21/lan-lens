# LAN Lens for OBS v0.2.9

## Full-frame portrait video

- Portrait preserves the whole camera frame at 1× zoom. Native tall frames keep their original proportions. When the browser supplies a wide frame, LAN Lens turns the entire frame into portrait.
- Portrait requests a native camera size where supported. The selected resolution continues to cap the shorter edge, and the app displays the resulting frame dimensions.

## Windows

Close OBS before running the unsigned Windows x64 MSI. Reopen OBS and choose **Docks → LAN Lens for OBS**. Requires the full OBS Studio 33.x build, version 33.0.1 or later.

Checked in portable OBS with a simulated camera. Physical phone framing and a full MSI installation remain unverified.
