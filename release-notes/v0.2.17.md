# LAN Lens for OBS v0.2.17

This update rolls up the changes since v0.2.1. The biggest additions are QR scanning from the installed phone app, a full-screen camera view, remote microphone control, and better OBS source management.

## Phone camera and pairing

- Install LAN Lens Camera on your Android or iPhone home screen. Scan the OBS pairing QR from inside the app or enter the six-character code. Pairing gives a clear message if a code was already used or a camera is active on that feed.
- Full-screen portrait has two centered rows of controls. Landscape puts the main toolbar at the bottom and shortcuts down the right side. Controls hide after three seconds of inactivity while capturing. Tap the preview to bring them back and adjust their opacity in **Full-screen settings**.
- Use smoother pinch-to-zoom or the full-width slider. Double-tap the preview or tap the lens switch icon to flip between front and rear. Switching back restores the last selected rear lens. Manual focus and exposure are available where the lens and browser support them.
- Portrait preserves the full camera frame at 1× zoom. Choose Auto, 360p, 540p, 720p, or 1080p. Auto adapts to the connection and encoder load. The controller reports the dimensions received by connected displays.

## OBS controller and displays

- Add feeds to the Preview scene in Studio Mode. LAN Lens avoids duplicate sources in the same scene, tracks sources when renamed, and reuses them across scenes. You can link a new feed to a vacant Browser Source and keep its existing scene references.
- Mute or unmute the feed microphone from the phone or controller. The dock follows the phone’s mic status and restores the control after a reconnect. Phones get a five-second warning before a host resolution change.
- Use **Identify all** to label connected displays. Waiting and signal-loss badges stay in the top corner. **Settings** contains **Open in Browser**, phone certificate download, and custom HTTPS. The phone’s optional camera statistics run while the panel is visible.
- New in v0.2.17, the dock’s dismissible **Buy me a coffee** button opens Ko-fi in the default browser. It appears at 10 AM local time and returns at 2 PM if dismissed. Dismissing it after 2 PM hides it until the next morning. [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/bitwraith21)

## Upgrade on Windows

Close OBS, then run **LAN-Lens-0.2.17-Windows-x64.msi** from **Assets**. Reopen OBS and choose **Docks → LAN Lens for OBS**. Requires the full OBS Studio 33.x build, version 33.0.1 or later.

The installer lists **bitwraith21** as the manufacturer. The MSI is unsigned, so SmartScreen may show **Unknown publisher**.

The portable OBS regression suite and MSI prerequisite checks passed. Full WiX validation and physical-device testing were outside this verification.
