# LAN Lens for OBS v0.2.3

## Camera and video

- Resolution now caps the outgoing video, even when the phone continues capturing at a higher resolution. OBS still displays the feed at the source size set in the scene. Auto resolution can change the outgoing cap based on connection statistics.
- The phone exposes automatic and manual focus and exposure on compatible cameras. Press and hold the preview to lock supported controls. Hold again or use the lock button to unlock. Available modes and adjustments depend on the phone, browser, and lens.

## OBS controller and displays

- Each feed has an accordion for display and camera commands. The card lists the bound OBS sources by their current names and the scenes that use them.
- In Studio Mode, Add to OBS targets Preview. Adding the same feed twice skips the duplicate. Adding it to another scene reuses the existing Browser Source. Deleting a feed clears its binding and keeps its existing OBS scene items.
- Waiting and signal-loss badges stay in the top-left corner. Their text scales with display width, and the status dot pulses unless reduced motion is enabled.

## Installation

Close OBS before running the Windows x64 MSI. Reopen OBS and choose **Docks → LAN Lens for OBS**. The full OBS Studio 33.x build, version 33.0.1 or later, is required. The MSI is unsigned.

The Windows build was checked in portable OBS using a simulated phone. Hardware focus and exposure behavior, a full MSI installation, and native macOS testing remain unverified.
