# LAN Lens for OBS v0.2.14

- The standard phone preview uses 60% of the viewport. The controls scroll below it in portrait and beside it in landscape, with separate accordions and thin scrollbars. The OBS dock and browser dashboard also use thin scrollbars.
- The toolbar cycles through Portrait, Landscape cropped, and Landscape. **Restart camera** moved into **Resolution**, and **Stop camera** is red while capture is active.
- Zoom has a full-width touch slider and smoother pinch and slider transitions. Cameras with coarse native zoom use smooth digital zoom from 1× to 4×.
- Full-screen camera actions sit above the lower edge and hide after three seconds of inactivity while capturing. Tap the preview to show them. **Full-screen settings** remembers the selected control opacity.

Close OBS before running the unsigned Windows x64 MSI. Requires the full OBS Studio 33.x build, version 33.0.1 or later.
