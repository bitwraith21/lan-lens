# LAN Lens for OBS v0.2.15

- Centered the two rows of phone controls in full screen. The camera toolbar sits above Settings, Exit full screen, Microphone, and Flashlight.
- Raised both rows to leave room for Chrome’s full-screen exit notice in portrait and landscape. The settings panel opens clear of the controls.

Close OBS before running the unsigned Windows x64 MSI. Requires the full OBS Studio 33.x build, version 33.0.1 or later.
